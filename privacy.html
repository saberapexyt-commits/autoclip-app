Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

AutoClip is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. We, the developers, do not operate a server for it and we do not receive, collect, store or sell your videos, clips, transcripts, TikTok data or any other personal information.

What stays on your PC

  • Your videos, generated clips, transcripts, settings and review decisions are stored in your Windows user profile.
  • Your TikTok sign-in tokens (for every account you connect) and any API keys you enter are stored encrypted for your Windows user (Windows DPAPI) and are never sent to us.
  • AutoClip has no AutoClip accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What AutoClip sends to other services

ServiceWhenWhat
TikTokWhen you connect an account or press UploadAutoClip talks directly to TikTok (open.tiktokapis.com) to sign you in, read your display name and open ID, and upload the clips you approved with the caption you wrote. TikTok's own privacy policy applies. AutoClip asks only for the permissions it needs (user.info.basic, video.upload).
This website / GitHubWhen AutoClip checks for updates, and when you download a releaseA request for releases.json and, if you update, the installer, from GitHub-hosted servers. These receive your IP address and the AutoClip version in the request, as any web request does. Nothing about your videos or accounts is included.
Anthropic (optional)Only if you add a Claude API keyA few still frames and transcript excerpts of candidate clips, to review them. Anthropic's terms apply and you pay Anthropic directly.
Hugging FaceWhen you download a speech model or the local AIA download request for the model files; no information about you or your videos.
PyPI / NVIDIAWhen you enable GPU accelerationA download of NVIDIA's CUDA runtime libraries, checked against their published checksum.
Twitch / KickWhen you paste a VOD linkAutoClip downloads the video (and chat, for Twitch) from them, like a browser would.

The website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party trackers, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. See the Cookie Policy. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in its privacy statement.

Your control

  • Disconnect a TikTok account in AutoClip (Settings or the Upload screen), or revoke AutoClip's access in your TikTok account settings, at any time.
  • Turn automatic update checks off under Settings → Updates.
  • Deleting %APPDATA%\AutoClip removes everything AutoClip stored about you, and uninstalling removes the program.

Children

AutoClip is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or requests: open an issue.
