Changelog

What's new in AutoClip

Every release, newest first. AutoClip tells you inside the app when a new one is available.

AutoClip 1.0.1

October 5, 2026
  • Fixed the taskbar and window icon so AutoClip shows its own icon.
  • Clip previews now use a still frame of the finished clip instead of a blank tile.

AutoClip 1.0.0

October 5, 2026
  • First public release: a native Windows desktop app (Home, Clips, Review, Upload, Settings) with a first-run setup.
  • Whole-VOD analysis from picture, audio, speech and chat; clips are cut to sentence boundaries and verified after rendering.
  • Speech transcription with word timestamps (Whisper), on your NVIDIA GPU when you enable it, otherwise on the CPU.
  • Free local AI frame model (downloaded on demand) and optional Claude review of candidate clips.
  • Review every clip before anything leaves your PC: approve, reject, rename, edit the caption.
  • Upload approved clips to TikTok through TikTok's official API, to one or several connected accounts, never twice by accident.
  • Automatic updates: signed releases, checked and downloaded in the background, installed only when AutoClip is idle.

The same information is published as releases.json, the file the AutoClip updater reads.
