Documentation

Everything you need to go from download to your first uploaded clip.

Installing AutoClip

  1. Go to the download page and download the installer.
  2. Run it. If Windows shows a blue SmartScreen box, click More info → Run anyway (the installer isn't code-signed yet; you can check the SHA-256 checksum shown on the download page).
  3. Choose whether to add a Desktop shortcut and finish. AutoClip installs for your Windows user only, so it doesn't need administrator rights.

Everything AutoClip needs is included, so there's no Python, ffmpeg or anything else to install. It does use the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime to draw its window, which is already part of Windows 11 and current Windows 10.

First-time setup

The first time you open AutoClip, a short wizard appears. Every step can be skipped and changed later in Settings.

  1. Your folders. Where you keep your VODs, and where finished clips should be saved (default: Videos\AutoClip).
  2. Speed and AI. AutoClip checks for an NVIDIA GPU and offers to enable GPU acceleration. You can also paste an optional Claude API key.
  3. Connect TikTok (optional). You can do this later from the Upload screen.

Adding a VOD

  1. Open Home.
  2. Paste a Twitch or Kick VOD link, or press Browse… and pick a video file (.mp4, .mkv, .mov, .ts, .flv, .webm and more).
  3. Press Start analysis.
Links need the video to be publicly viewable. For subscriber-only or deleted VODs, download the video another way and choose the file instead.

Generating clips

While AutoClip works you'll see which stage it's on (downloading, watching the video, listening to the audio, transcribing, reviewing candidates, cutting clips). It can be cancelled at any time. When it finishes, it opens the Review screen.

You can tune the defaults in Settings → Clip defaults:

SettingWhat it does
Clips to findThe most clips to make per VOD.
Lead-in / aftermathHow many seconds of context before the action and after it.
FormatVertical zoom (cropped to the action), full frame (whole picture with blurred bars), or fill (full-screen crop).
Thorough scanExamines the whole VOD with every signal. Turn it off for a faster, picture-only scan.
Twitch chatUses chat activity (KEKW, POG, CLIP spikes) as a signal.
Burn captionsAdds subtitles from the transcript to each clip.

If part of the analysis couldn't run, for example no speech was found or chat wasn't available, an orange notice tells you which stage and why. The clips are still made from what did work.

Reviewing clips

The Clips screen shows everything AutoClip made, with a preview (hover to play), a score, the length and a status. Click a clip to open Review.

  • Approve (A) or Reject (R). Use ← → to move between clips.
  • Title and caption: edit them and press Save title & caption. Renaming a clip renames its file as well. The caption is what will appear on TikTok.
  • You can approve or reject several clips at once from the Clips screen.

Nothing is uploaded until you approve a clip and press Upload.

Connecting TikTok

AutoClip uploads through TikTok's official API. You sign in on tiktok.com in your own browser; AutoClip never sees your password. TikTok requires a free developer app to do this, which you create once:

  1. Go to developers.tiktok.com, sign in, and create an organization and an app.
  2. In the app, fill in the details (name, icon, description, category, Terms and Privacy URLs, website) and add the products Login Kit and Content Posting API.
  3. Under Login Kit → Redirect URI → Desktop, add exactly: http://127.0.0.1:38125/callback/
  4. While the app is in sandbox, add yourself (and each friend) as a target user.
  5. Copy the app's Client key and Client secret into AutoClip → Settings → TikTok (or import a small file containing them) and press Save.
  6. On the Upload screen press Connect TikTok. Your browser opens TikTok; approve AutoClip, then return to the app.

Several accounts: press + Add another account on the Upload screen as many times as you like, and pick which account a batch of clips goes to.

Drafts versus direct posting

ModeWhat happensNeeds
Inbox (default)Clips arrive as drafts in your TikTok inbox. You open the TikTok app, add sound or hashtags, and press Post.Any approved or sandboxed developer app
DirectClips are published straight to your profile.TikTok must audit your app. Until then every direct post is private ("only me")

TikTok limits uploads to about 6 requests a minute and 5 unfinished drafts per day. AutoClip waits out rate limits and shows TikTok's own message if you hit the draft limit.

Uploading clips

  1. Approve your clips in Review.
  2. Open Upload, choose the account, and tick the clips you want (approved clips are ticked for you).
  3. Press Upload selected.

The queue shows each clip as Waiting, Uploading, Processing, Uploaded or Failed with the reason. A failed clip can simply be uploaded again. AutoClip won't upload the same video to the same account twice, even if you renamed it; Upload again asks for confirmation if you really want a second copy.

GPU setup

With an NVIDIA graphics card, Settings → GPU acceleration → Enable downloads NVIDIA's CUDA libraries once (about 1.3 GB, checked for integrity, stored in your user folder) and transcription then runs several times faster. It also makes the most accurate Whisper model (Best) practical.

  • You need a current NVIDIA driver. No GPU, or a problem with it? AutoClip silently uses the CPU instead.
  • GPU use → Off disables it entirely. Always also uses the GPU for video decoding and encoding, which is usually not faster.
  • Pick the Whisper model under Settings → AI: Automatic, Fast, Balanced or Best.

Local AI & Claude

AutoClip works without any AI account. To let an AI look at real frames of each candidate clip:

  • Local AI (free, private): Settings → AI → Local AI → Download (about 305 MB, once). Press Test to confirm it works. It scores frames but doesn't read the transcript.
  • Claude (optional): paste your Anthropic API key under Settings → AI. A few frames and transcript excerpts per candidate are sent to Anthropic; you pay Anthropic directly. It reads the transcript and writes real titles.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
The window opens blank or doesn't openInstall the WebView2 Runtime and restart AutoClip.
"Download failed" for a linkOpen the link in your browser to make sure the VOD is public. Otherwise download the video yourself and choose the file.
Orange notice: [transcription]Speech wasn't used. Read the message: no speech in the video, a model couldn't load, or no audio track. Settings → AI → Test checks transcription.
Clips are all called "Big explosive play"That's the free local AI's labelling. Add a Claude key for real titles, or rename clips in Review.
GPU shows "Enable" even though you have oneThe one-time library download hasn't been done, or the NVIDIA driver is outdated.
TikTok says the redirect URI doesn't matchAdd exactly http://127.0.0.1:38125/callback/ as a Desktop redirect URI in your TikTok app.
Port 38125 is in useAnother program is using it. Close it and press Connect TikTok again.
Upload failed: rate limitAutoClip waits and retries automatically; if it still fails, wait a minute and upload again.
Something elseSee Support, and attach %APPDATA%\AutoClip\autoclip.log.

Updating AutoClip

AutoClip checks for new versions when it starts and every few hours. When one is found:

  • A small notice appears: Update now, Later or Skip. The update downloads in the background and is verified (every release is digitally signed).
  • It's never installed while AutoClip is analysing a VOD, making clips or uploading. If you choose Update now during work, it waits until the work finishes.
  • By default a downloaded update also installs when you close AutoClip. Change this under Settings → Updates.
  • Your settings, accounts, projects and clips are kept.

Under Settings → Updates you can check manually, switch to the Beta channel, turn automatic checks off, or install an older version if a release causes trouble. If an update is interrupted, AutoClip tells you and offers to retry.

Your data & uninstalling

%APPDATA%\AutoClipSettings, encrypted keys and TikTok logins, projects, the upload history, downloaded models, the log file.
Videos\AutoClip (or the folder you chose)Your finished clips.
The program itself%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\AutoClip

Uninstalling (Windows Settings → Apps) removes only the program. Your clips and settings stay unless you delete those folders.
